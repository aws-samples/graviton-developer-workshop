# Sample Prompts for Crypto Trading Agent

Try these example prompts to explore the trading agent's capabilities:

## Portfolio Overview

- **Show my current portfolio positions**
  View your current holdings, quantities, and current market values.

## Risk Analysis

- **Calculate the 30-day VaR for my portfolio at 95% confidence**
  Computes Value-at-Risk using historical simulation to estimate potential losses.

## Options Pricing

- **Run a Black-Scholes calculation for a BTC call option expiring in 30 days, strike 70000**
  Prices a European call option using the Black-Scholes model with current market data.

## Correlation Analysis

- **Analyze the correlation between BTC and ETH over the last 30 days**
  Calculates the Pearson correlation coefficient and visualizes price movements.

## PnL Reporting

- **Calculate my PnL breakdown by asset for this month**
  Shows realized and unrealized profit/loss for each asset in your portfolio.

## Technical Analysis

- **What are the 20-day and 50-day moving averages for BTC? Is there a golden cross?**
  Computes simple moving averages and identifies bullish/bearish crossover signals.
